[[link](https://www.routledge.com/Generalized-Linear-Mixed-Models-Modern-Concepts-Methods-and-Applications/Stroup-Ptukhina-Garai/p/book/9781498755566?srsltid=AfmBOop80SBSwTFMCIzkiTtYe-5uir_Xnw2KVZxa1oXb4LJWrLRx0Wwq)] | *Note: First edition of this book has a very nicely written Chapter 2!*
